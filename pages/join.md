# Get an account

One account opens every service that needs one.

1. Ask someone who has an account for an invite code, or send a request from the sign-up page.
2. Choose a password on the page the link opens.
3. Sign in to the services with it.

There is no email address at sign-up, and no reset by email: if you lose your password, ask the
people who run dlhost for a new link.
